I’m Rishi, a designer who likes calm, useful products and the research behind them. I turn messy problems into clear flows, simple screens and visual systems that people can use without a manual.

I work across mobile apps, web dashboards and visual design. I like being involved early, when the questions are still open, and staying through to the details of the handoff.

Process

How a project usually runs. The steps overlap, and I go back to research whenever a test surprises me.

  1. 01

    Research

    Talk to the people who will use it, look at what already exists, and write down what is really getting in the way.

  2. 02

    Define

    Turn the findings into a short brief: who it is for, the main jobs to be done and what a good outcome looks like.

  3. 03

    Design

    Flows first, then wireframes, then high-fidelity screens and a clickable prototype to try them in.

  4. 04

    Test & ship

    Usability tests on the prototype, fixes, then tidy specs and components handed over to development.
